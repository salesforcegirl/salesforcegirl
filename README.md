### Hi, I'm Randi 👋

Salesforce platform and GTM systems leader (SOX, CPQ, DevOps), building on Salesforce since 2007. I take the friction out of how teams work, and I build my own tools on the side. *See a need, fill a need.*

**What I'm building**

- **[SaasyLogs](https://saasylogs.com)**: paste or upload a Salesforce debug log into a Google Sheet and see the path your save took. Free on the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/saasy_logs/726192293541).
- **[DwelLogs](https://dwellogs.com)**: a free app for keeping a pulse on your home: jobs, check-ups, readings, and what it all costs.
- **[DeployLogs](https://deploylogs.com)**: a deploy runlist and go/no-go tracker in Google Sheets, for deploys that cross teams.
- **[Rubber Duck Games](https://rubberduckgames.com)**: free little learning games for curious kids: letters and sounds, counting, typing and quick math, played right in the browser.
- **[PayZoneMap](https://payzonemap.com)**: which pay zone am I in? Published US pay zones from each company's own pages, side by side and mapped by county.
