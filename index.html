Why I build

What drives the work

01

See a need, fill a need.

Every project I take on starts with a real problem, whether it's mine, my team's, or my family's. SaasyLogs came from fighting Salesforce debug logs. DwelLogs came from family asking for a simple way to keep up with their homes. DeployLogs came from years of running cross-team deploys from a spreadsheet.

02

Be the change you want to see.

If a tool should exist and doesn't, I'd rather build it than wait for someone else to. That goes double for keeping useful things simple, affordable, and respectful of people's data.

03

To reach the stars, you have to aim for them.

The only sure way to fail is to never try. I pick up new tools and stacks by building something real with them, and I aim past what I already know how to do.

04

Work smarter, not harder.

If I have to do something twice, I look for a way to automate it. Good tooling, AI included, frees up time for the problems that actually need a person.

Projects

What I'm building, and why

I design, build, and ship all three of these myself, from idea to launch.

SaasyLogs

Free · Google Sheets add-on

Paste or upload a Salesforce debug log into a Google Sheet and see the path your save took: every trigger, flow and class, in order. Then what went wrong, and where the time went.

The problem

Debug logs are huge, noisy, and hard to read. Finding the one line that matters eats time on every bug.

Why I built it

I live in these logs, so I built the tool I want on every bug. It isn't on the Workspace Marketplace yet; you can ask for access on the site.

DwelLogs

Free · web app

Keep a pulse on your home: the filters, the well, the animals, the thing that broke last week. What your home needs and when, shared with everyone who lives there.

The problem

Home upkeep lives in scattered notes, receipts, and memory. Homeowners, condo owners, and renters all lose track of what's due and what it'll cost.

How I'm approaching it

  • Free and simple first; scale up only as needed
  • Low running costs by design
  • Careful handling of user data from day one
  • Sign in with a code from your email, no passwords
  • Works offline, for places with no signal
  • Add it to your Home Screen; no app store needed
dwellogs.com → GitHub → The story →

DeployLogs

In testing · Google Sheets

A deploy runlist and go/no-go tracker for Google Sheets, for deploys that cross teams.

The problem

Deploys fail in the gaps between teams: steps kept in someone's head, IDs one team creates that the next one needs, and estimates nobody has ever run.

Why I'm building it

I've run cross-team deploys from a spreadsheet tracker for years. Heroics are not a deploy plan, so the tracker is becoming an app.
